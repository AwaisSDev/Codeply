Questions.

Ambient intelligence for your computer and phone. It's a family of apps on one account: Craft on your desktop, Crew for bots, Orbit for voice, Mobile on your phone and the CLI in your terminal. Ask it something or give it a job, and it gets on with it.

Yes. Craft is free, and it includes Cloud runs and Crew. Auto comes with a daily allowance, and you can bring your own models and keys whenever you like.

Not by default. Every write, edit and command asks first, with the change in front of you. If you'd rather it just ran, you can turn approvals off.

With Cloud on, the task moves to the cloud and keeps going. When it's done it opens a pull request, and you can check it and merge it from your phone.

Orbit is voice mode. Talk to Codeply out loud and it hands the work to Craft or a Crew bot. Bots can call you too, when something needs a decision.

Auto picks a model for each step for you. You can also connect OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, DeepSeek or a local model through Ollama.

Same agent, different home. The CLI lives in your terminal. Craft is the desktop app with chats, projects, Cloud, Crew and a browser panel that checks its own work.

Craft runs on Windows 10 and 11 and on macOS, with separate builds for Apple silicon and Intel. Updates install themselves.
