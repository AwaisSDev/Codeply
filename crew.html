Codeply Crew

Build a crew of AI bots that work for you. Describe one in plain words, give it a voice, and let it take care of the inbox, the calendar and the follow-ups.

Get Craft See how it works

Built into Codeply Craft, with the same crew on your phone.

Describe it, get a bot

Write what the bot should do and how it should talk. Crew builds it from that prompt, or you can start from a template.

Put your bots in one chat

Start a group chat with several bots and ask once. Each one answers from its own job, and you can @ the one you want.

It can call you

Pick a bot and call it like a friend, or let it ring you. It talks in a natural voice, with live captions so you can follow along.

Every bot is its own

Give each one a look, a voice and clear limits.

Sticker avatar

A custom sticker for every bot, so you know who is talking at a glance.

Its own voice

Each bot has a voice you pick, and it uses it when it calls you.

Memory

It remembers what you told it, so you don't have to repeat yourself.

Approval boundary

You decide what it may do alone and what waits for your yes.

Help with the everyday

The small jobs that fill a day, handled.

Reminders

Ask for a nudge and it reminds you at the right time.

Plan my day

One ask and you get a plan for the day ahead.

Gmail drafts

It drafts replies in Gmail. You edit them before anything is sent.

Google Calendar

It works with your Google Calendar, so plans fit around what is booked.

Keeps working when your PC is off

Your bots carry on in Codeply Cloud while your computer sleeps, so nothing waits for you to log back in.

The same crew everywhere

The bots you build here also work in Craft, in Studio and in the mobile app.
