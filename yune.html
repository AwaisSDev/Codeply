Frequently asked questions

What is Yune?

Yune is your personal AI companion. It learns what you like, notices what’s coming up and takes care of it: bookings, rides, email, plans and code. It works in the background, so you don’t have to keep prompting it.

How does Yune remember things?

It picks things up from your chats and the apps you connect, like the seat you always choose or the friend who doesn’t eat mushrooms. You can see, change or delete anything it remembers.

Will it do things without me asking?

It can start on its own, like spotting your flight and lining up a ride. But anything that sends, books, pays or changes something waits for your yes.

What are specialists?

Focused agents with their own skills and memory. Yune comes with a team, and it can build a new specialist whenever you need help with something specific.

Can I schedule things?

Yes. Morning briefs, weekly reports, reminders, checks before a trip. Set a time once and Yune runs it for you, even when your laptop is closed.

Can it really code?

Yes. Code mode in the app and Yune Code in your terminal read your project, make changes, run the tests and check the result in a real browser before calling it done.

How much does Yune cost?

Yune is free to start.
