Small things that make it work

It keeps hearing you

On iPhone, a watchdog wakes the mic if iOS quietly stops it, so nothing you say gets lost.

Light on memory

Whisper loads only when you use voice and unloads after 5 quiet minutes. Codeply starts at about 430 MB instead of 1.15 GB.
