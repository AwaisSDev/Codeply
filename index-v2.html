Why we built Yune

Most AI waits for you to come back and type the next thing. We wanted one that just gets on with it, and checks its work like a good teammate would.

So we made Wisp warm and friendly, gave it a team of specialists, and made sure nothing important happens without your yes.

Frequently asked questions

What is Yune?

Yune gives you Wisp, one AI agent with a team of specialists behind it. It handles your inbox, calendar, business and code, and keeps going in the cloud when you leave.

What can I ask Wisp to do?

Sort and answer email, plan your week, research options, write documents and build or fix code. Wisp picks the right teammate for each part.

What’s the difference between Yune Code and Yune CLI?

Yune Code is the desktop app for Windows and macOS. Yune CLI is the same agent in your terminal. Same account, same memory, same team.

Does it work when my laptop is closed?

Yes. Send a task to the cloud and it keeps going on its own, then shows you what it did when you’re back.

Will it do anything without asking me?

No. Every write, edit, email and command waits for your yes, with the change in front of you.

How much does Yune cost?

Yune is free to start.
