Questions, answered.

What is Codeply?

One personal AI agent, Wisp, that works at your desk, keeps going in the cloud when you leave, and calls you when it needs you. It handles your inbox, calendar and code from one place.

Does it work when my computer is off?

Yes. Click Cloud and the run keeps going on its own. You can check it, review it and merge it from your phone.

Will it do anything without asking me?

No. Every write, edit, email and command waits for your yes, with the change in front of you. You can undo a whole message in one click.

Which models does it use?

Haze picks a model for each step automatically. You can also bring OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, or run a local model on your own PC.

How much does it cost?

Codeply is free to start on Windows, macOS, iPhone, Android and in the browser.
