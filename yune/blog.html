Notes from Yune

What we’re building, and why.

Notes

Yune is AI that’s around you

For most of this year, our story was “agents that ship while you sleep”. That was true, but it only described one part of what we do. The bigger idea is simpler: AI should be around you, not waiting in a tab.

So that’s the story now. Yune is AI that’s already on it, on your computer and in your terminal.

What that looks like today

  • Yune works on your PC. It builds the feature, checks it in a real browser and fixes what’s broken before it hands it back.
  • The cloud is one click away. The task keeps going while your PC is off, then merges into main.
  • Voice mode lets you talk it through. It answers out loud and gets the real work done.
  • One shortcut, any app. Alt + C brings Yune into whatever you’re doing, so you never switch windows to ask for something.
  • A team behind it. Yune brings in teammates for the inbox, the calendar and the follow-ups, and waits for your yes.
  • Yune Code is the same agent in your terminal.

What’s next

More of Yune showing up where you already are, and less of you going to it. If you try it, tell us what you think at mawais9171@gmail.com.
