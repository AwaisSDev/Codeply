Frequently asked questions

What is Yune?

Yune is one AI agent with a team of specialists behind it. It handles your inbox, calendar, business and code, and keeps going in the cloud when you leave.

What can I ask Yune to do?

Sort and answer email, plan your week, research options, write documents and build or fix code. Yune picks the right teammate for each part.

What’s the difference between Yune and Yune Code?

Yune is the app for Windows and macOS, with a Chat mode for everyday work and a Code mode for your projects. Yune Code is the same agent in your terminal. Same account, same memory, same team.

Does it work when my laptop is closed?

Yes. Send a task to the cloud and it keeps going on its own, then shows you what it did when you’re back.

Will it do anything without asking me?

No. Every write, edit, email and command waits for your yes, with the change in front of you.

How much does Yune cost?
