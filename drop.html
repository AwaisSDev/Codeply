Step 1
Install and sign in
Download the installer and sign in with an email code. No API keys to manage.
Step 2
Hit the hotkey
Drop sits quietly in your system tray. Hit your hotkey and paste what you copied from an AI chat, or type what you want.
Step 3
It lands in the right file
Drop finds the right file and applies a small verified diff. It changes only what is needed, never a full-file rewrite.
