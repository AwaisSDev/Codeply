Built into Codeply Craft

Your coding agent,
one shortcut away.

In any app, Craft drops into the corner of your screen. Give it a task, watch every step, and allow a deploy with one click, without opening the full window.

Get Craft for WindowsHow it works

Alt+C on Windows · Option+C on Mac · Included with Codeply Craft

How it works

One hotkey. The whole agent.

Install Craft once

Craft starts with your computer and waits in the tray, so the drop is ready even with the Craft window closed. Turn that off from the tray icon.

Press the shortcut and type

Alt+C on Windows, Option+C on Mac. Pick a project, or just say "make me a portfolio site" and Craft makes the folder. Choose Build or Ask, the model and the permissions, then send.

Watch, allow, undo

Each step shows as it happens. Before a deploy the drop asks first. When it's done you see what changed, with Undo, and Open in Craft takes the chat into the full app.

Had the old Drop app? Craft replaces it.

Uninstall Codeply Drop, install Craft, and the same shortcut opens the new drop.
