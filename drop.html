Download the installer and sign in with a simple email code. No API keys to manage, no complex configuration.

2
Trigger the Hotkey

Codeply sits quietly in your system tray. When you need a change, hit your hotkey and paste what you copied from your AI chat.

Surgical Application

Codeply identifies the right file and applies a verified diff. It changes only what's necessary, avoiding full-file rewrites.
