What's new in Codeply

Everything we've shipped, newest first.

New · Nov 1, 2026

One name: Codeply

  • Craft is now Codeply. The desktop app, the drop, voice mode (Orbit), VS Code (Studio) and the phone app are all one product, called Codeply.
  • Crew is now Codeply Wisp. The same agents you make in a sentence, that work around the clock and call you when they need a yes.
  • The CLI is now Codeply CLI. Same agent, same account, in your terminal.
  • One logo. Everything carries the blue Codeply icon.
Shipped · Oct 30, 2026

Codeply 2.3: one personal agent, the top bar, a new look

  • One name: Codeply. The window, tray, sign-in, quick chat, voice and the phone apps now all say Codeply. Craft, Crew, the drop, Orbit and Cloud are features of one product: the app switch reads Code, Agents and Design, and Crew is now Agents.
  • One agent, quiet specialists. Codeply is a single personal agent. Your bots become its private specialists: it hands them work in their area, can make a new one for something you do again and again (up to 12), and speaks as itself. Put @Name in a message to ask one directly.
  • A top bar that turns into the chat. A thin bar rests at the top of your screen. It becomes a waveform while Orbit listens or talks, shows what it heard and said, breathes while a task runs, and one click turns it into a minimal chat. Alt+C (Option+C on a Mac) opens the same chat. It fades over full-screen apps and stays out of screen recordings.
  • A proof ledger. Every email sent and calendar event made is written down before it runs and checked after, so a retry never sends a second email or adds a second event. Every chat now shares the same memory, your day and what Codeply did lately.
  • A morning message. Built from that ledger and sent at the time you choose, with a suggested focus block in a free stretch of your day.
  • A new look. A redrawn icon set of 91 icons, calmer stepped dark surfaces, bigger type, a wider composer and a blue send button.
  • A tidier box. The + button now holds files and photos, Goal, the three modes, Research and Cloud. This Computer and Cloud sit on top of the sidebar, and the rest of your folders sort A to Z or stay where you drag them.
  • More slash commands. /new, /model, /effort, /usage, /agents, /loop, /schedule, /review, /security (finds loopholes, ranked, with fixes) and /judge (guesses the real goal, argues for it, then attacks it and gives a verdict).
  • CLI 0.7.0. The CLI help and agent personas now say Codeply too.

Codeply Crew on iPhone and Android

  • Your agents, as a phone app. Each agent is a chat thread you can message or call, with a page showing what each one knows. Share a screenshot, a photo or your PC's screen and the agent sees it.
  • Calls and texts that reach you. When an agent calls or texts you, or a reminder comes due, it arrives as a phone notification. Upcoming ones ring even with the app closed, and texts land in the chat. Offline chats can set reminders too.
  • Hearing on Android. Calls now listen through the phone's own speech recognizer, so your agent hears you properly.
  • Edge to edge. The app draws under the status and navigation bars.
  • Install it today. An installable Android APK and an iPhone build, both built on GitHub.

Craft 2.2.0 and CLI 0.6.0: a faster engine, docks like Claude's

  • A new engine under the same app. Craft's chats now run on the DSH harness, with Craft's own edit checks, shell hints, undo, web search and a browser check added, and Haze 2 built in. It also lists your own models.

The shared brain

  • One personal context for every app and bot. Personal memory, project context, short-lived live context and team lessons live in one place. Jokes, sarcasm and hypotheticals are ignored, first mentions are tentative, your corrections stick and secrets are never kept.
  • Agent handoffs. A bot hands work to the right bot with just the intent, the facts and the decision. A meeting request in the email bot goes to the calendar bot, with no loops.

  • Just suggest, never act. A new autonomy level for bots that should only make suggestions.
  • Gmail and Calendar, fuller. CC on emails, whole-email reads, and calendar guests and edits, tested end to end against five simulated busy people. Health now shows up in Life too.

Codeply Motion and the drag + Shift converter

  • Codeply Motion. A headless edit and motion studio that agents drive with short commands, so they can cut video and render motion graphics. Builds for Windows and Mac.
  • Drag + Shift converter. Drag any file in Windows, hold Shift, and a ring of formats appears under your mouse. Drop on one and a converted copy lands next to the original. It handles images (PNG, JPG, WEBP, AVIF, TIFF, BMP, HEIC in), PDF to images, and Word to PDF and back.

Craft 2.0.6: Design, cloud handoff and life bots

  • Cloud handoff. If your PC drops, the run carries on in Craft Cloud from where it was, and when your PC is back the changes merge and it carries on. A per-project switch, on by default.
  • Life bots. Bots share a life context (calendar, Gmail, calls, plans and projects) and reach out by themselves with a daily budget and quiet hours: leaving soon, before a meeting, an email waiting for a reply.
  • Bots with their own email and Slack. Each bot can have its own address, and Slack messages to your bots become tasks.
  • A call helper. It listens to calls on your PC, shows cards and whispers, and can take a call over as your assistant.
  • Autopilot. Tasks from email, Slack, calls and chat run, get checked and are reported back, with ask, hold or full autonomy per bot and a Stop all bots button, also on the phone.
  • Life, always on. It listens in the background and reads your screen, from a simpler Life page with big type and a one-button start.
  • Design. Checks once or twice instead of after every edit, and design folders never turn into Craft projects.

Just launched

Codeply, now in your terminal

  • A real coding agent, not just a script runner. The Codeply CLI reads your codebase, edits and creates files, searches for patterns, runs shell commands, and checks its own work before moving on, the same job tools like Claude Code do, right in your shell instead of a browser.
  • One line to install. No npm knowledge required:
    irm https://codeply.app/install.ps1 | iex (Windows)
    curl -fsSL https://codeply.app/install.sh | sh (macOS / Linux / WSL)
  • Same account. Sign in with codeply login and it uses the same account as the desktop app. Prefer to bring your own model instead? codeply provider ollama switches to a local or cloud Ollama model.
  • Still scriptable. codeply apply "<instruction>" --file path/to/file for a single one-shot edit, no interactive session needed.
Shipped

Built-in AI, no API key required

  • Sign in and go. Codeply now runs on a built-in AI engine, no more pasting in a provider API key or picking a model. Sign in with your email, get a code, and you're editing.
  • Smarter caching. Identical requests against an unchanged file are now instant and free. Codeply remembers what it already worked out instead of re-asking the AI.
  • Better disambiguation. When the same property or value shows up in more than one place in a file, Codeply now uses the surrounding context to find the one you actually meant, instead of guessing.
  • Onboarding that remembers you. The "where'd you hear about us" and country questions are now tied to your account, not your device. Switching accounts on the same computer won't skip them.
Shipped

Intelligent file detection & multi-file placement

  • Paste and go. Copy code from anywhere and Codeply figures out which file it belongs in, even across multiple files in one paste, using project structure, framework conventions, and a search of your codebase's own identifiers.
  • Surgical edits. Changes land as small, verified diffs instead of full-file rewrites: faster, cheaper, and far less likely to touch anything you didn't ask about.
  • Local response cache. A per-project cache (.codeply/) keeps recent AI answers and file history on disk, with automatic expiry and a size cap so it never grows unbounded.
